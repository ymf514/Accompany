Self-talk — an uninterruptible monologue

Triggered with a 35% chance each time the idle timer crosses into "long quiet" or "very quiet", and again on each of "very quiet"'s 12-second repeats. Mood jumps to 100 the moment it starts, then decays normally like always. A line is built by picking one of four topics (movie / food / weather / a half-remembered feeling), then an opening line, sometimes a middle line, and a line that trails off without finishing — some slots inside these lines are filled in from a separate word pool, so exact wording varies. Nothing you do is ignored outright: an interaction during self-talk gets a light acknowledgement, but at most once every 5 seconds, and never a full reaction. The first real interaction after it finishes gets pulled back to you instead of its usual line.

you know, there was this movie—
self-talk opener (movie)
it wasn't even that good, but there was this one part—
self-talk middle (movie)
anyway—
self-talk trailing line (several topics)
one sec / almost done / just a moment / let me finish this—
light acknowledgement during self-talk, max once per 5s
oh—right, you were saying? / wait, what were you saying? / sorry, go on—what was that? / oh, right. you.
first real interaction after self-talk ends
